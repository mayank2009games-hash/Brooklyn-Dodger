Brooklyn Dodger – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Summary

Brooklyn Dodger does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings, coins, unlocked cars, stars, and best scores locally on your device so you can continue later. This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no ad trackers
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location

Motion sensors

If you choose the optional “Tilt” steering mode, the game reads your device’s motion sensor only to steer the car. This information is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
